Sponsor questions

Open items to confirm

before final assumptions

  2. What are the battery manufacturing emissions for the retrofit kit?
  3. Can the project scope stay focused on F-150 only?
  4. When vehicle lifespan is extended by 10-15 years, does the battery last that long too?
  5. Are there any other costs associated with maintaining the retrofit?
